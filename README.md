# youtube-tips

this is a "guide" i put together in like ~20 minutes, and it’s pretty much just a list of tips i found from across the internet

---

## inside-youtube

1. don’t upload different niches
2. don’t upload inconsistently
3. like and respond to comments (boosts engagement)
4. create an end screen chain (Video 1 → 2 → 3 → 4) ((longer watch time))
5. use community tab (attracts more people to your channel)
6. analyze when your competitors upload, and when they get the most views (so you can get "inspiration" for when you upload)
7. ignore the audience tab until you get more subscribers (NOT the community tab. audience tab is where you find which times your audience watches you at.)
8. don’t do sub4sub. you look like an idiot. (can we point and laugh?) ((it KILLS your engagement ratio. this is because the people you sub to are likely not going to watch your video, and you’re not gonna watch theirs. it’s a suicide pact.))
9. titles (subsection) ((make sure they pop out. idk how to describe it well, so just research others))
   + titles that ask questions
   + result + intrigue (e.g., *How I Made CRYSTAL METH In Real Life...*)
10. don’t do compilations until your channel is monetized.
11. use tools like vidiq. (if somebody reaches out to me with a FOSS alternative, hit me up and I’ll change this)
12. don’t just copy other people. nobody likes a clone.
13. train the algorithm to like you. by this I mean you should subscribe to your competitors, and engage (like, comment, watch their videos) with them for a couple hours a day, for 2–3 days straight.
14. don’t drop your videos in Discord group chats. this is suicide. (YouTube sees this and doesn’t count it towards engagement)
15. don’t delete videos. your viewers won’t trust you.

## thumbnails

1. limit the objects in the thumbnail. no more than 3.
2. always have closeups. make them big and bold.
3. don’t be a lazy fuck. spend time on your thumbnails, and if you have an idea, ALWAYS follow through. unless it’s dogshit. then don’t.
4. use thumbnail testing. it’s great. makes it so that you can technically have more than one thumbnail to multiple different viewers.

---

## editing

1. don’t be personal in the beginning, nobody cares. (wait until it is relevant to your video’s story, then you can do so. but only once it is relevant)
2. be suspenseful, because people really, and I mean REALLY like it when you edge them on, but don’t do this unless you do it properly. (don’t edge them on for too long. this isn’t the bedroom.)
3. don’t say "watch until the end". it’s desperate.
4. don’t repeat jokes.
5. do short intros, and make the topic clear.
6. be funny (resets attention span).
7. have good fonts (bad fonts make people turn away).
8. analyze where your viewers leave from your videos, and then apply that to your own video. add transitions, but make sure they keep the flow of the video.
9. don’t add a channel intro into every video.
10. hide easter eggs for commenters (more engagement).
11. use music (don’t just drag and drop some random mp3 file. you should cut to the beat, place music where there are cuts. matching the video energy is REALLY important. fade the music in/out.)

## shorts

### just making it clear that i personally HATE shorts. this is because they are pretty much cheating, and there is no art in making them. if you are fine with either of those, continue. this "guide" is for people who want to learn more with youtube, not a checklist for me.

1. most of the time, you don’t need thumbnails. if your audience comes from the shorts feed, you can skip it altogether.
2. make titles for the algorithm, not the audience.
3. don’t use trending tags (it confuses the algorithm, and fucks up your niche).
4. even in shorts, stick to your niche.
