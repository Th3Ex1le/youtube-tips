# youtube-tips
this is a "guide" i put together in like ~20 minutes, and its pretty much just a list of tips i found from across the internet



## INSIDE YOUTUBE

1 - dont upload different niches
2 - dont upload inconsistently
3 - like and respond to comments (boosts engagement)
4 - create an end screen chain (Video 1 → 2 → 3 → 4) ((longer watch time))
5 - use community tab (attracts more people to your channel)
6 - analyze when your competitors upload, and when they get the most views (so you can get "inspiration" for when you upload)
7 - ignore the audience tab until you get more subscribers (NOT the community tab. audience tab is where you find which times your audience watches you at.)
8 - dont do sub4sub. you look like an idiot. (can we point and laugh?) ((it KILLS your engagement ratio. this is because the people you sub too are likely not going to watch your video, and your not gonna watch theirs. its a suicide pact.))
9 - titles (subsection) ((make sure they pop out. idk how to describe it well, so just research others))
	- titles that ask questions
	- result + intrigue (e,g, How I Made CRYSTAL METH In Real Life...)
10 - dont do compilations until your channel is monetized.
11 - use tools like vidiq. (if somebody reaches out to me with a FOSS alternative, hit me up and ill change this)
12 - dont just copy other people. nobody likes a clone.
13 - train the algorithm to like you. by this i mean you should subscribe to your competitors, and engange (like, comment, watch their videos.) with them for a couple hours a day, for 2-3 days straight.
14 - dont drop your videos in discord group chats. this is suicide. (youtube sees this, and doesnt count it towards engagment)
15 - dont delete videos. your viewers wont trust you.


## THUMBNAILS

1 - limit the objects in the thumbnail. no more than 3.
2 - always have closeups. make them big and bold
3 - dont be a lazy fuck. spend time on your thumbnails, and if you have an idea, ALWAYS follow through. unless its dogshit. then dont.
4 - use thumbnail testing. its great. makes it so that you can technically have more than one thumbnail to multiple different viewers.


## EDITING

1 - dont be personal in the beginning, nobody cares. (wait until it is relevant to your videos story, then you can do so. but only once it is relevant)
2 - be suspenseful, because people really, and i mean REALLY like it when you edge them on, but dont do this unless you do it properly. (dont edge them on for too long. this isn't the bedroom.)
3 - dont say "watch until the end". its desperate.
4 - dont repeat jokes.
5 - do short intros, and make the topic clear
6 - be funny (resets attention span)
7 - have good fonts (bad fonts make people turn away)
8 - analyze where your viewers leave from your videos, and then apply that to your own video. add transitions, but make sure they keep the flow of the video
9 - dont add a channel intro into every video
10 - hide easter eggs for commenters (more engagement)
11 - use music (dont just drag and drop some random mp3 file. you should cut to the beat, place music where there is cuts. matching the video energy is REALLY important. fade the music in/ out.


## SHORTS
### just making it clear that i personally HATE shorts. this is because they are pretty much cheating, and there is not art in making them. if you are fine with either of those, continue. this "guide" is for people who want to learn more with youtube, not a checklist for me.

1 - most of the time, you dont need thumbnails. if your audience comes from the shorts feed, you can skip it altogether.
2 - make titles for the algorithm, not the audience. 
3 - dont use trending tags (it confuses the algorithm, and fucks up your niche)
4 - even in shorts, stick to your niche.
